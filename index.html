Reading the categories

Represented (E) is a positive result: a claim traceable to Lomiiel already sits on the work. Missing registration (A) is where a composition he evidently wrote has no work or no claim traceable to him. Duplicate work (B) and recording linkage (C) are data-relationship problems: his claim exists but sits on a record that cannot match the recording’s usage. Writer attribution (D) is where the label credits him and the registry does not. Each recording appears in exactly one category.
How to read this report. Findings describe the condition of the public registration record on the report date. “Represented” is a positive result. “Missing registration”, “duplicate work”, “recording linkage” and “writer attribution” describe metadata and registration conditions that can be corrected; none states that money is owed, unpaid or lost, and none infers ownership percentages. Plays are shown to indicate where exposure is concentrated.
