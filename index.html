Reading the categories

Represented (E) is a positive result: his interest is represented on the record carrying the recording and nothing else is outstanding. It does not mean every other represented song is perfect — a song can be represented and have a linkage or duplicate problem, in which case it sits under that problem with its representation shown in the song record. Registration gap (A) is no work, or a work with no representation of his interest. Duplicate / conflict (B) and recording linkage (C) are data-relationship problems. Writer attribution (D) is where his exact identity is not on the record — where Cuero Macho / EMPIRE representation is present that is a confirmation, not a missing claim.
How to read this report. Findings describe the condition of the public registration record on the report date across four independent questions: representation, attribution, linkage and work status. None states that money is owed, unpaid or lost, and none infers ownership percentages. Plays are shown to indicate where exposure is concentrated.
